# Cranial nerve teaching example

Download `neuroanatomy-lab.pptx` and `objectives.xlsx`, then choose **Build from slides + objectives** in Lesson Builder. The workbook references the MRI on slide 23. Review the selected image, educator answers, source, and licence before creating the lesson. The 12 objectives cover High school, Undergrad, Step 1, Post-Step 1, Step 2, and Resident. Audience mappings are new curriculum adaptations, not board-exam certification.

## Teaching limitations

The supplied finding is left facial nerve enhancement with facial weakness. The source predicts forehead involvement for this peripheral lesion. It does not establish a specific disease cause. The adaptation has not received independent clinician review.

Extracted raster media do not preserve PowerPoint arrows, overlaid text, or cropping. The original 320-pixel MRI is suitable for discussing the source-described finding; do not invent pixel coordinates or require detailed independent localization beyond its resolution. Slide 24 and speaker notes contain instructor answers and should remain outside the learner's media selection.

Objective evidence is formative feedback. Assisted same-case performance does not establish independent retention, transfer, or prevention of deskilling.
