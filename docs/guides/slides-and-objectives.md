# Create a coached lesson from slides and objectives

Choose **Create a lesson** on the home page. Add a PowerPoint and an Excel or CSV objective table. CaseAttend reads both in your browser, lets you select the teaching images, and builds a lesson around your supplied answers.

## Try a published teaching example

On **Create a lesson**, choose **Use MRI example** or **Use illustration example** to load the paired teaching files and their source credits in one action. Both examples still require you to review the selected images and answer keys before creating a lesson. You can also download their files and use the upload controls.

- **MRI:** [Wake Forest neuroanatomy lab slides](https://caseattend.com/teaching-examples/cranial-nerves/neuroanatomy-lab.pptx) and [matching objective workbook](https://caseattend.com/teaching-examples/cranial-nerves/objectives.xlsx). The original deck is from Lack et al., MedEdPORTAL (2022), under CC BY 4.0. The CaseAttend workbook has 12 objectives across six audiences, all referencing the MRI on slide 23. [Credits and limitations](https://caseattend.com/teaching-examples/cranial-nerves/README.md).
- **Illustration:** [Original cranial-nerve lecture](https://caseattend.com/teaching-examples/facial-branches/original-lecture.pptx) and [matching objective workbook](https://caseattend.com/teaching-examples/facial-branches/objectives.xlsx). Six adapted objectives reference slide 48. The facial-branch illustration is by Patrick J. Lynch and C. Carl Jaffe, from Wikimedia Commons under **CC BY 2.5**; the surrounding course is CC BY 4.0. Preserve the separate image credit and licence. This is an anatomy illustration without a supplied patient diagnosis. [Credits and limitations](https://caseattend.com/teaching-examples/facial-branches/README.md).

1. **Add both files.** Choose the PowerPoint and objective table, then select **Review my material**, or use an example button.
2. **Choose the material.** Review the objective rows and select the relevant images. Answer slides and speaker notes belong to the educator, so review the media selection before showing it to students. PowerPoint arrows, crops and overlays may be absent; labels embedded in the image remain visible.
3. **Confirm and create.** Give the lesson a neutral title and description, review the answer key, and supply author, source and licence credit. Create the lesson, export a portable copy, and open it to rehearse.

Your answer key is the source of truth for coaching. It should state the known diagnosis or finding, relevant location, rationale and limits. You do not need a model to invent a diagnosis from your media. The tutor adapts its questions and language to the selected level.

## Objective table

Use a visible worksheet called **Objectives**, with one objective per row.

| Column | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Level | High school, Undergrad, Step 1, Post-Step 1, Step 2, or Resident |
| Objective | What the student should demonstrate |
| Expected evidence | What a satisfactory student explanation contains |
| Answer key | The educator's known facts and reasoning, including uncertainties |
| Hint | An optional prompt that helps without supplying the full answer |
| Slides | Original PowerPoint positions, such as `23`, `8, 9`, or `16–17` |
| Source URL | An optional HTTPS teaching source for that row |

The first four columns are required. Different levels can have different objectives in the same workbook. Select no more than 24 objectives per level so each can receive an evidence check; split larger curricula into separate lessons. Slide references must exist in the visible deck and match selected teaching media. Formula cells use their saved values; CaseAttend does not calculate formulas or run macros.

## What the student sees

Guided practice starts with a question and a place to explain an observation. Hints are explicit actions. Cached answer-reveal buttons are suppressed. The **Objective evidence** column shows formative observations with exact learner excerpts and whether help was used. Objective numbers keep answer-bearing objective titles private.

The coach and optional objective checks use the student's connected OpenRouter account. **Objective checks start off.** Select **Check objective evidence** in the lesson to enable them. They use a separate paid model and the student's OpenRouter balance, even if the coach model is free. Coaching can continue with checks off. An unavailable or invalid check is **Not assessed**, never invented credit.

A response after help remains assisted. A new attempt on the same material is practice; another case and a delayed assessment are needed to evaluate transfer and retention. These features do not establish prevention of deskilling. [Research informing the design](../development/guided-practice-evidence.md).

## Save and reuse

The saved lesson includes the selected re-encoded images, educator answer keys, level-specific objectives, sources and exact linked case revision. Original source files and speaker notes are not automatically copied into the portable lesson. The import does not confer a clinical-review or de-identification attestation.

If storage is available, the lesson is saved in this browser. In memory-only mode, export the portable copy before opening the lesson or closing the page, and check that the download reached your device. If saving fails, export remains available; **Retry save** must succeed before the lesson can open from browser storage. The portable copy contains the instructor answer keys. Use **Use an existing case instead** to reach the detailed Lesson Builder, or [Case Studio](create-a-case.md) to work from image files directly.
